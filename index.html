Demos

Three browser-only demos by Ian Mauck. Nothing is uploaded, no account is created on a server, and every portfolio, fund, manager and letter is synthetic; the few published figures are cited where they appear.

Demo 01

Allocate

A close-the-month tool for allocators: what can become cash and by when, where the portfolio sits against policy, what changed at the managers, and a review with a memo.

Open Allocate ›
Demo 02

Manager Monitor

Reads a fund manager's quarterly letter, extracts what matters, cites the sentence behind every value, and drafts the change memo. Scripted mode, no calls to any AI service.

Open Manager Monitor ›
Demo 03

Fund Sim

A seed fund portfolio construction simulator: set the checks, the price, the reserves and the follow-on rule, and it runs thousands of funds on power-law outcomes to show the spread of net multiples, when cash comes back, and what reserves a strategy actually needs. Illustrative assumptions, not market data.

Open Fund Sim ›

All portfolios, funds, managers and letters here are synthetic, every fund parameter in Fund Sim is an illustrative assumption, and nothing describes a real institution, fund, manager or client. Nothing here is investment advice.
